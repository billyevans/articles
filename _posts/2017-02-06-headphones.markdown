title: Headphones
---

Here you can find my very subjective feedback on headphones I was used in my life.  
Everything has started with MD Sony player I have been using with pair of Sennheiser PX 100 in 2003 year.  

1. Apple ear pods ★  
Better than nothing. Could be used only for calls as a headset. Do not use it for music listening.  
Terrible sound quality, not existing sound insulation. Works with Macbook as headset.  
![]({{ site.github.url }}/assets/headphones/180D4A1B-CD14-455C-AEC4-06C893791546.png)

2. Sennheiser PX-100 ★★  
Pretty cheap headphones. However, they gives much better sound quality than Apple ear pods. Light and foldable with convenient case.  
Sound insulation is pretty bad. Cheap student's option. Discontinued for a few years already and no replacement exists.  
![]({{ site.github.url }}/assets/headphones/FE7DF712-5933-440E-988C-C69966A8F2B9.png)

3. Sennheiser PX-200 ★★☆  
A bit better than PX-100. Closed-back headphones, hence better sound insulation.  
![]({{ site.github.url }}/assets/headphones/2A7AACB3-CB15-4387-A469-EAC518D6AC00.png)

4. [AKG K450](https://www.akg.com/K450.html) ★★★  
These headphones are a step up from the PX-100/PX-200 models and come with several accessories, including two cables of different lengths and a carrying case.  
However, the cables are difficult to replace due to their unique connection to the headphones, and both tend to wear out within 1.5 years.  
![]({{ site.github.url }}/assets/headphones/2BD863E5-E72B-4FC7-B5DD-7F422087F2D7.png)

5. [Audio-Technica ATH-ES7](https://www.audio-technica.com/en-us/ath-es7) ★★★★  
These are pretty good headphones. They lasted me about 4-5 years with relatively active use.  
The cable near the jack wore out after about 2 years. I replaced it, but then the cable near the ear cups started to fail — I had to resolder it.  
But now the ear pads are completely worn out. The sound isolation is decent, and the headphones are closed-back.  
However, they always pressed uncomfortably on my head from the headband. I had the white ones, and they got quite dirty.  
They’re no longer produced, and the Audio-Technica ATH-ES700 model has replaced them. The new model has a much softer headband, which I think should fix the issues I had.  
![]({{ site.github.url }}/assets/headphones/8C67FEE6-84D7-419A-832B-46D00213ECA6.png)

6. [Sony MDR-7506](https://electronics.sony.com/audio/headphones/all-headphones/p/mdr7506?srsltid=AfmBOooPN1AS6u0XDsFf61XfRfiBM5XkJ2rZRwByGFNgSdNXKR3czr27) ★★★★  
Very comfortable ears. The sound is somewhat bland, but quite good. Large, clearly not for outdoor use. They seem to be considered professional studio headphones. They live at work.  
Reliability and comfort is pretty good. Also they are cheap.  
![]({{ site.github.url }}/assets/headphones/1A14BA67-04C5-4259-BFA2-C4DC55E2F85F.png)

7. Beyerdynamic DX 160 iE ★★★☆  
My first in-ear headphones that I bought myself. Turned out to be pretty good.  
The cord is convenient and easier to untangle. Comes with a good set of accessories. The sound is a bit odd with excessive bass, but overall, not bad.  
![]({{ site.github.url }}/assets/headphones/CCA9E750-5097-4F7E-97B5-9239E33FFC12.png)

8. [Hifiman re-400](https://www.hifiman.com/products/detail/113) ★★★★☆  
Great headphones for their price of £50(in 2024 it costs $20). It seems like you can't get anything better for that money.  
The sound is quite good, noticeably better than Beyerdynamic. The sound isolation is also good.  
However, the build quality isn't great. With these, I tried foam tips for the first time and realized I can no longer use silicone ones at all.  
I'm already on my third pair since they consistently die after a year.  
![]({{ site.github.url }}/assets/headphones/1DE487B7-AD7C-4AE2-8BAE-6E22D08C913F.png)

9. Plantronics backbeat Pro ★★★★☆  
Wireless. Packed with features. The sound is good. They last long; I charge them about once every two weeks.  
I have found only two downsides. Sometimes the buttons don't work. And these headphones are heavy and big.  
They also have position sensors to automatically pause and resume playback, but they sometimes triggered falsely, so I disabled them.  
There's active noise cancellation, which works pretty well. Bluetooth is reliable, with only a couple of disconnects in six months of use.  
Another plus is that if the battery dies, you can just connect them with a cable. They also work decently as a headset.  
These are my first wireless headphones, and surprisingly, the sound is good, quite comparable to the Sony MDR-7506. I was using them as main headphones for quite a while.  
For this price, you won’t find this kind of functionality. There's an updated model now where they’ve improved the noise cancellation, weight, and size.  
![]({{ site.github.url }}/assets/headphones/16E5E26E-60B6-4669-B5EF-8AA7A558AD53.png)

10. [Shure SE215](https://www.shure.com/en-US/products/earphones/se215?variant=SE215-CL) ★★★★★  
In terms of sound, these are absolutely the best headphones I’ve ever come across(to year 2017).  
They outperform all the others I’ve used. The sound isolation is also excellent. The fit is a bit unusual at first, and the cable is stiff.  
But other than that, these are the best headphones for music that I’ve bought! A plus is the detachable cable. There’s also now a model for iPhone with a headset and remote.  
![]({{ site.github.url }}/assets/headphones/91ADD449-5BE4-4829-A4BF-34F5435916A0.png)
